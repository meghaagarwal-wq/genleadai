# ARIA — MARKETING SHEET (designer-ready)

**Purpose:** A copy-and-paste-ready marketing pack for landing pages, ads, decks, one-pagers, and social. Every block is production copy — no filler, no code references. Hand this to a designer, they can drop it into Canva / Figma / Framer / Webflow without rewrites.

**Brand voice cheatsheet:**
- Sentences are short. Verbs first.
- Never call ARIA a "bot," "tool," or "assistant." She's **ARIA** — refer as a person, use **"she."**
- Palette: Deep founder green `#0F4C3A` · warm coral `#E06D53` · cream `#FDFBF7` · charcoal `#1C1917`
- Typography: Display = **Outfit**, Body = **Manrope**, Mono = **JetBrains Mono**
- Emoji: minimal, favor iconography (Phosphor icons in-product)

## 18. FAQ (for footer, help center, and RFP responses)

**Q: Does ARIA send anything without my approval?**
A: No — not by default. Every draft waits in Reply Triage until you approve. If you *want* autopilot for specific signals (e.g. "auto-reply to any lead with score ≥ 90"), you can flip that switch yourself.

**Q: How does ARIA learn my voice?**
A: She scrapes your website, LinkedIn, and past emails on setup. That builds her Training Profile — the voice + positioning model behind every draft.

**Q: Does she work with my CRM?**
A: Yes. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Close, Freshsales — 54 integrations total.

**Q: What if I need to take over a conversation?**
A: Kill switch is one click. ARIA pauses for that lead. Resume anytime.

**Q: What about privacy and tenant isolation?**
A: Every read and write is tenant-scoped. Audited February 2026. Enterprise plans include SSO, region-selectable data residency, and a dedicated instance.

**Q: What model does ARIA use?**
A: Claude Haiku 4.5 (via Anthropic). Enterprise plans can bring their own model.

**Q: How fast is setup?**
A: 5 minutes to sign up. First hour: ARIA ingests your site and past emails. First morning: you approve her first drafts.

**Q: What does ARIA cost?**
A: Founder tier from $99/mo. Team tier from $499/mo. Enterprise is custom.
